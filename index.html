What we do

One character. Many bodies.

Most assistants forget you after every chat. Our characters keep a stable identity, remember what matters, and stay the same person in an app, on a screen or inside a robot.

Character development

Consistent personality, values and voice. Each character has its own story, habits and daily rhythm.

Long-term memory

Characters remember people, promises and important moments, and bring them up at the right time.

Robot integration

A simple API turns a character's decisions into robot actions: speak, look, follow, fetch and carry.

How it works

From a sentence to an action

The character thinks with a large language model through an API. Our bridge layer turns that thought into safe, simple commands for the robot.

1
Listen & seeThe robot's phone and camera hear speech and recognise faces.
2
ThinkThe character, powered by an LLM API, decides what to say and what to do, using its memory.
3
ActThe bridge checks safety rules and sends commands to motors and the screen face.
